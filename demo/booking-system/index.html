Try it in 60 seconds

The full loop, exactly as it works for a real shop:

  1. Book as a guest. Open the Customer view, add a treatment or two, pick tomorrow and a start time, choose a therapist (or No preference), fill in any name and email, and confirm.
  2. Open the Owner view. Your booking is sitting in “Waiting for confirmation” — the shop also gets an email alert in the real system.
  3. Confirm it. If you chose “No preference”, assign a therapist first (the card suggests one), tap Confirm, and watch it move into Upcoming. Then explore: the calendar, Phone Booking, Schedules, and the records report.

What’s inside

Every feature below is live in this demo — including the availability rules.

  • Step-by-step guest booking, up to 5 treatments in one visit
  • Real per-therapist availability — schedules, days off, one-off shift changes
  • “No preference” bookings the owner assigns on confirm
  • Two-guest treatments need two free therapists
  • Pending queue with one-tap Confirm / Cancel / Done / Restore
  • Phone bookings with a live availability calendar
  • Treatments & prices the owner can edit
  • Staff roster: weekly hours, days off, custom shifts
  • Per-therapist day lists — print or email
  • Blocked dates for shop closures
  • Website messages inbox
  • Booking records & revenue report, printable + CSV
