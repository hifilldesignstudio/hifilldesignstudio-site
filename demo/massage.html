The studio

A quiet room, a practical plan.

Our light-filled treatment rooms are designed to feel calm without feeling precious. Your therapist will check in about pressure, temperature, positioning and music so you can settle in comfortably.

After the session, you will leave with clear, manageable next steps—whether that means a simple stretch, a follow-up plan or simply more rest.
