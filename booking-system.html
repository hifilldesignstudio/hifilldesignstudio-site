What's inside.

Everything an appointment-based shop needs day to day — nothing it doesn't.

Guests book themselves

Step-by-step booking on your website, up to 5 treatments in one visit — even while you're with a customer, or after closing time.

Automatic emails

You, your staff and your guest are notified automatically. Guests get their own private link to reschedule or cancel — fewer phone calls, fewer no-shows.

Your own dashboard

Confirm, cancel or complete bookings in one tap. Phone bookings go into the same calendar, so a time slot is never sold twice.

Real staff schedules

Weekly hours, days off and one-off shift changes per staff member. The system only offers times that are actually free.

Your services, your prices

Records & reports

Daily lists per staff member, booking history and revenue reports — printable, or exported to CSV.
