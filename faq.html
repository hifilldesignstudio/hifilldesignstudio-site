Frequently asked questions.

Straight answers before you get in touch.

Is the website really only CAD $59?

Yes. The Starter-Lite package is a one-time CAD $59 for a professional one-page website — mobile-friendly, with a contact form, basic SEO, a simple logo design and 2 rounds of revisions. Domain, hosting and email are not included.

Are there any hidden fees?

No. The price you see is the price you pay. If you need anything beyond the package — for example extra pages — we'll quote it clearly before any work begins.

What do you need from me to get started?

Your final text, photos and logo. Once we receive everything, your first draft is delivered within 3 working days.

How many revisions do I get?

Two full rounds of revisions are included in the Starter package, so you can fine-tune the design until it feels right.

What's the difference between Starter and Starter-Plus?

Starter (CAD $99) is the website itself — you arrange your own domain and hosting. Starter-Plus (CAD $199) is the complete package: website plus a standard domain for the first year, business email, first-year web hosting, SSL certificate, website backup and 30 minutes of small updates each month. After the first year it's CAD $150/year.

Can you build an online store?

Yes. WooCommerce online stores start from CAD $399 and Shopify store setup starts from CAD $499 — quoted separately based on what you need to sell.

Can customers book appointments on my website?

Yes. Quote request forms, appointment forms and booking journeys can be built into your website — tell us what your business needs in the inquiry form.

What does basic SEO include?

Every website includes the SEO foundations: descriptive page titles and meta descriptions, a mobile-friendly structure, fast-loading pages, and Google Search Console setup so Google can find and index your site. Need more, like keyword research or ongoing optimization? Mention it in your inquiry and we'll talk options.

Do I own my website?

Yes — once it's paid for and launched, the website is 100% yours.
